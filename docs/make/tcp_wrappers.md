# tcp_wrappers 7.6 (binary only) - DEPRECATED
  - Package: [master/make/pkgs/tcp_wrappers/](https://github.com/Freetz-NG/freetz-ng/tree/master/make/pkgs/tcp_wrappers/)
  - Steward: -

TCP Wrappers provide a security measure for service
[daemons](http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daemon)
such as `ftpd` by standing between the daemon and incoming service
requests. TCP Wrappers log successful and unsuccessful connection
attempts. They also provide
[access control](http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Access_Control_List)
by allowing or denying a connection depending on the origin of the
request. TCP Wrappers are used to protect daemons such as SSH, Telnet,
and FTP.

In Freetz, this package is currently required by the NFS server.
