# portmap 6.0 (binary only) - DEPRECATED
  - Package: [master/make/pkgs/portmap/](https://github.com/Freetz-NG/freetz-ng/tree/master/make/pkgs/portmap/)
  - Steward: -

The **portmap** service is a dynamic port-assignment daemon for
[RPC](http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remote_Procedure_Call) services such
as
[NIS](http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_Information_Service)
and
[NFS](http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_File_System).
It has weak authentication mechanisms and can assign a wide range of
ports for the services it controls.

Among other things, *Portmap* is required by the [NFS](nfs.md) package.
